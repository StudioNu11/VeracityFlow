# VeracityFlow

VeracityFlow is a desktop fact-verification assistant that helps users evaluate the credibility of information without interrupting their workflow. With a single global hotkey, VeracityFlow captures a temporary screenshot of your screen, identifies the primary claim being viewed, gathers relevant evidence, and presents a transparent credibility assessment.

Instead of requiring users to manually copy and paste text into a fact-checking website, VeracityFlow brings verification directly to the content you're viewing, making it faster, more accessible, and easier to use in everyday situations.

---

## Features

* Global hotkey for instant verification from anywhere
* Temporary screenshot capture for seamless claim detection
* Automatic extraction of the primary claim from on-screen content
* Evidence collection from multiple online sources
* Trust Rating and Confidence Score for every verification
* Transparent reasoning explaining every assessment
* Supporting and conflicting evidence presented side by side
* Lightweight overlay that minimizes interruptions
* Clean, modern desktop interface designed for speed and usability

---

## How It Works

1. Press the global hotkey while viewing content on your screen.
2. VeracityFlow captures a temporary screenshot of the current display.
3. The primary claim is identified from the captured content.
4. Relevant evidence is collected from multiple sources.
5. The scoring engine evaluates the available evidence based on credibility, relevance, consistency, and quality.
6. A lightweight overlay displays the verification results without disrupting your workflow.

---

## Verification Output

Each verification includes:

### Trust Rating

An overall measure of how trustworthy the claim appears based on the available evidence.

### Confidence Score

Indicates how certain the assessment is, taking into account the quality and consistency of the evidence.

### Reasoning

A concise explanation describing the key factors that influenced the final assessment.

### Evidence

A collection of supporting and conflicting sources used during the evaluation, allowing users to understand both sides of the claim.

---

## Designed for Everyday Use

Traditional fact-checking often requires opening new tabs, copying text, searching manually, and comparing multiple sources before reaching a conclusion.

VeracityFlow streamlines this entire process into a single action. Whether you're reading an article, scrolling through social media, watching a video, or browsing the web, verification is always just one keyboard shortcut away.

The lightweight overlay keeps you focused on your current task, providing the information you need without forcing you to leave the application you're using.

---

## Design Principles

### Accessibility

Verification should be available whenever it's needed. The global hotkey and lightweight overlay ensure that information can be evaluated with minimal effort.

### Transparency

Every assessment is accompanied by clear reasoning and the evidence used to reach it, enabling users to understand the evaluation rather than simply accepting a verdict.

### Reliability

Instead of relying on a single source, VeracityFlow evaluates multiple pieces of evidence to produce a balanced and informed assessment.

### Efficiency

By automating claim extraction and evidence gathering, VeracityFlow significantly reduces the time required to verify online information.
